# {{name}}

It's the roaring 1920s, and young college student Laura Bow has been invited by her friend Lillian for a weekend getaway to her uncle, Colonel Henri Dijon's estate, for a family reunion deep in Southern Louisiana, where they plan to discuss his new will. As soon as Laura arrives, she's confronted with the backstabbing, lies, and secrets of the Dijon family - until the first body turns up. Laura realizes she has no choice but to stay hidden and uncover the murderer before she becomes the next victim.

## Location

The !screenshot:bathroom can be found on the second floor in the plantation house.

## Points

* &full; As a house guest, you have access to a well-furnished bathroom.
* &presence; When using the toilet, an amusing message is shown.
* &presence; You may wash your hands, but not before your friend Lillian does.
* &presence; Laura may take a shower, triggering an "!easter:psychotic" easter-egg.
