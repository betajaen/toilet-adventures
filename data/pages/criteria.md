# Point Rules

* Toilet/Sink/Bathtub/Shower does not have a presence, or may not be directly interacted with by the protaginst, usage is implied. 0.5 pt.
* Toilet/Sink/Bathtub/Shower has a presence which may interacted with, in the form of dialgoue, animation or a cutscene, 1 pt
* Use of the bathroom, or bathroom furniture directly, and consquently results in a bug, but does not crash the game, 1pt.
* Use of the bathroom, or bathroom furniture directly, crashes the game, 2 pt.
* Full working bathroom, that can be used frequently through out the majority of the game, 1 pt.

# Game Critera

* Game was published between 1980 to 1995
* Game has a continous story with narrative
